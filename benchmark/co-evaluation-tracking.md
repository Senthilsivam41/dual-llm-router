# Co-Evaluation Tracking

| Metric | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Variant Fitness | Composite score per variant combo |
| Selection Pressure | How much better new variants are vs. old |
| Diversity | Number of unique variant combinations alive |
| Convergence | Are we settling on one best combo? |
| Cost Reduction Trend | Is cost going down over generations? |
| Quality Improvement Trend | Is quality going up over generations? |
